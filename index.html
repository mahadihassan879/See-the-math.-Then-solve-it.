    Steps for the stuck

    Every solution is cut into four or five short moves, each explained in plain words. Nobody gets lost halfway down the page.

    Every problem gets a shape

    Surfaces, solids and vectors appear beside the working. Students see why the answer is true, not just what it is.

    Algebra to calculus

    Start with a parabola in Year 9 and keep going to partial derivatives and volumes in university. One tool the whole way.

    Abstract maths is hard to hold in your head.

    Most students don't fail because they can't calculate. They fail because they can't picture what the symbols mean. Dimensa pairs every worked solution with a model they can rotate, zoom and touch, so the idea finally clicks.
